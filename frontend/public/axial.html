Worked example & model assumptions

A 50 kN pull on a 500 mm² steel bar gives σ = F/A = 100 MPa. With E = 200 GPa, ε = σ/E = 0.0005. Over 2 m, ΔL = εL = 1 mm of elongation.

Tension gives positive stress, strain and length change. A lower modulus increases strain and elongation while stress stays fixed. This model assumes centered loading, a uniform cross section and linear elasticity. It excludes buckling, yielding and stress concentrations.
