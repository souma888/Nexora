Worked example & sign conventions

For σx = 80 MPa, σy = 20 MPa and τxy = 40 MPa, the center is 50 MPa and the radius is √(30² + 40²) = 50 MPa. The principal stresses are 100 MPa and 0 MPa. The major principal angle is about 26.565° counterclockwise from x.

Normal stress is positive in tension. Positive τxy acts in +y on the +x face. The circle plots positive shear upward, so rotating the element counterclockwise moves its x′ face point clockwise by twice the angle. Maximum shear here is in-plane.
