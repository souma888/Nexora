Worked examples

All three examples use a 6 m span, E = 200 GPa and I = 8,000 cm⁴, giving EI = 16 MN·m².

Center point load, 24 kN. Symmetry gives 12 kN at each support. At midspan, PL/4 = 36 kN·m and −PL³/(48EI) = −6.75 mm.

Uniform load, 24 kN/m. The total load is 144 kN, so each support carries 72 kN. At midspan, wL²/8 = 108 kN·m and −5wL⁴/(384EI) ≈ −25.313 mm.

Cantilever tip load, 24 kN. The fixed support carries 24 kN vertically. The internal moment there is −PL = −144 kN·m, and tip deflection is −PL³/(3EI) = −108 mm.

Model notes & sign conventions

The solver uses closed-form Euler–Bernoulli beam equations. The beam is prismatic with constant EI and assumes small deflections and linear elastic material. The specified load acts downward. Self-weight is excluded.

Position x increases to the right. Reactions are positive upward, sagging moment is positive, shear follows dM/dx, and downward deflection is negative. Peak values retain the sign at the largest absolute magnitude.

V = dM/dx, EI·d²δ/dx² = M, and θ = dδ/dx. Under a point load with unchanged force and EI, peak deflection grows with L³. Under a uniform load with unchanged load per metre and EI, it grows with L⁴.
